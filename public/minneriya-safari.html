Discover Minneriya National Park

A private car taxi service explores grasslands and wooded areas around Minneriya Reservoir. Elephants often move in family groups near the shore.

The park also supports deer, buffalo, monkeys, crocodiles and birds. Water levels and the season influence what you might see.
Sri Lankan Elephants
Look for elephant families feeding in open areas beside the reservoir. Group sizes change with the season and water levels.
Wild Buffalo
Buffalo graze on the grassy plains and can be seen cooling down in the shallows.
Spotted Deer
Spotted deer may be grazing in a clearing or standing at the woodland edge.
Crocodiles
Check the shoreline and shallows, where crocodiles sometimes rest.
Birdlife
Herons, pelicans, peafowl and eagles are among the birds found near the reservoir and woodland edge.
