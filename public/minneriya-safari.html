Discover Minneriya National Park

A private jeep safari explores grasslands and wooded areas around Minneriya Reservoir. Elephants often move in family groups near the shore.

The park also supports deer, buffalo, monkeys, crocodiles and birds. Water levels and the season influence what you might see.
Sri Lankan Elephants
Look for elephant families feeding in open areas beside the reservoir. Group sizes change with the season and water levels.
Wild Buffalo
Buffalo graze on the grassy plains and can be seen cooling down in the shallows.
Spotted Deer
Spotted deer may be grazing in a clearing or standing at the woodland edge.
Crocodiles
Check the shoreline and shallows, where crocodiles sometimes rest.
Birdlife
Herons, pelicans, peafowl and eagles are among the birds found near the reservoir and woodland edge.

Minneriya — Location & Safari Access

Illustrative map — not for navigation.
Minneriya lies near Habarana and Sigiriya. Safari routes cross woodland and grassland near the reservoir.

Hotel collection and return are included in Habarana and Sigiriya. Share your hotel so we can set a pickup time. Ask about the fare from other locations.

Janaka recommends Minneriya, Kaudulla or Hurulu Eco Park according to where elephants are moving before your visit.

Your Safari Experience

Mr. Janaka with guests and a safari jeep.
Travel with a driver-guide in a private jeep around the reservoir. Look out for elephant herds, deer, buffalo and birds.

Each jeep carries a pair of binoculars. Wildlife sightings depend on seasonal conditions and the animals’ movements.

Both morning and evening departures include bottled water and transfers from hotels in Habarana and Sigiriya.

Send your date and hotel to Janaka, who can confirm pickup and the park that suits current elephant movements.
