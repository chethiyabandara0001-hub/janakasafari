Why Choose Us for Your Safari Tours?

01. Experience You Can Trust
Our guides started running safaris in 2002 and launched Nisha Safari Jeep Tours in 2006.
02. Experienced Local Team
Four English-speaking local guides lead the tours. Each holds a licence from Sri Lanka’s Department of Wildlife Conservation and knows the parks and their wildlife.
03. Thoughtfully Planned Safaris
Safaris run in 4×4 jeeps. The team plans for early entry and chooses quieter routes when conditions allow.
04. Respect for Wildlife & Nature
Our guides follow park rules and give animals space while helping you observe them in their natural habitat.
