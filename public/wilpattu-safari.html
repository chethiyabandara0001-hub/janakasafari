Discover Wilpattu National Park

Drive the woodland roads around Wilpattu’s natural lakes, known as villus, in a private car. Leopards and sloth bears use the woods and the lake margins.

The park is also home to elephants, deer, crocodiles and many bird species. What you encounter depends on the conditions and the animals.
Sri Lankan Leopards
Leopards may cross forest tracks or shelter in shade near the natural lakes.
Sri Lankan Sloth Bears
Sloth bears forage on the forest floor and at woodland edges. Their movements determine where you might encounter them.
Deer & Forest Wildlife
Spotted deer and sambar may feed in a clearing or move from the forest to the lake edge.
Crocodiles
Check the shoreline and shallows, where crocodiles sometimes rest.
Birdlife
Look for eagles, storks, peafowl and other birds at forest edges and around the natural lakes.
