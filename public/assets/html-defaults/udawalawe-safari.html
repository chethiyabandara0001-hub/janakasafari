Discover Udawalawe National Park

In Udawalawe, private jeeps travel across open country and scrub beside the reservoir. Elephant families feed, move between grasslands or enter the water to bathe.

There may also be buffalo, deer, crocodiles, monitor lizards and birds such as peafowl, eagles and kingfishers. Encounters vary from trip to trip.
Sri Lankan Elephants
Look for elephant families along the reservoir shore or crossing the open grasslands.
Water Buffalo
Water buffalo can be seen grazing or cooling off in muddy pools and shallow water.
Spotted Deer
Spotted deer may be grazing in a clearing or standing at the woodland edge.
Crocodiles
Check the shoreline and shallows, where crocodiles sometimes rest.
Birdlife
Watch the open country and the reservoir for eagles, peafowl, kingfishers and waterbirds.

Getting to Udawalawe

The park straddles the Ratnapura and Monaragala districts around Udawalawe Reservoir. The main gate is on the road towards Thanamalwila.
