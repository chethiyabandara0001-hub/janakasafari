Discover Bundala National Park

Bundala’s coastal lagoons and scrub form a Ramsar-listed wetland. Resident waterbirds share the area with birds passing through seasonally.

Elephants, spotted deer and crocodiles also live here. Water levels, season and animal movements all affect what you may see.
Waterbirds & Wetland Life
Pelicans, herons and painted storks often feed in shallow water or rest beside the lagoons.
Sri Lankan Elephants
Wild elephants move between the open country and patches of scrub in Bundala.
Spotted Deer
Spotted deer may be grazing in a clearing or standing at the woodland edge.
Crocodiles
Check the shoreline and shallows, where crocodiles sometimes rest.
Seasonal Migratory Birds
Look for visiting species on mudflats and lagoons. The birds present change throughout the year.
