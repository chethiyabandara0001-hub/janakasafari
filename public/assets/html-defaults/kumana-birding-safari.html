Discover Kumana National Park

Kumana combines woodland, lagoons and wetlands. Along with waterbirds, look for Sri Lankan leopards, elephants and other animals.

Janaka suggests Kumana if you prefer a quieter park. Visitor numbers and wildlife sightings vary with conditions.
Waterbirds & Wetland Life
Scan the edges of Kumana’s lagoons for herons, pelicans, painted storks and other waterbirds.
Sri Lankan Elephants
Elephants travel through scrub, forest and open areas. Encounters depend on where the herds are that day.
Spotted Deer
Spotted deer may be grazing in a clearing or standing at the woodland edge.
Sri Lankan Leopards
Leopards can move along forest roads and rest around exposed rock. Sightings depend on where the animals travel.
Seasonal Migratory Birds
Look for visiting species on mudflats and lagoons. The birds present change throughout the year.

Getting to Kumana

Kumana is on Sri Lanka’s southeastern coast, within reach of Arugam Bay. The park has woodland as well as lagoons and wetlands.
