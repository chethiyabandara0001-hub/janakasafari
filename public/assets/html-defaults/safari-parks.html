Find Your Sri Lankan Safari

Choose from six Sri Lankan national parks. Each page lists the safari options and practical details for planning a trip around the wildlife you hope to see.
Kumana Safari
Look for leopards, elephants and birds on quieter jeep routes through Kumana’s woodland and wetlands.
VIEW SAFARI
Yala Safari
Travel through Yala’s varied terrain in a 4×4 and watch for leopards, elephants, sloth bears and other animals.
VIEW SAFARI
Udawalawe Safari
Take a jeep into Udawalawe’s grasslands and scrub to look for elephant herds near the reservoir.
VIEW SAFARI
Bundala Birding Safari
Go birding along Bundala’s coastal lagoons to see resident waterbirds and, in season, migrating species.
VIEW SAFARI
Wilpattu Safari
A 4×4 safari follows forest tracks and lake shores in Wilpattu, with opportunities to spot leopards, sloth bears and birds.
VIEW SAFARI
Minneriya Safari
Watch for wild elephants near Minneriya Reservoir, where herds may gather during the dry season.
