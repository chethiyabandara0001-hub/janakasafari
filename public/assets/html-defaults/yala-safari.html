Discover Yala National Park

Yala’s landscape ranges from dry forest and grassy plains to rocky outcrops and lagoons near the coast.

Sri Lankan leopards share the park with elephants, sloth bears, spotted deer, crocodiles and many birds. A guided 4×4 trip crosses these habitats. The conditions and wildlife movements shape each visit.

Sri Lankan Leopards
Keep an eye on rocky ledges and scrub for Sri Lankan leopards resting or moving through cover.
Wild Elephants
Elephant families feed in open areas and sometimes meet near the waterholes.
Sloth Bears
Sloth bears may come into view as they forage on the forest floor.
Tuskers
Tuskers are among the elephants that visitors may encounter in Yala.
Birdlife
Look for bee-eaters, eagles and other birds as the jeep passes forests and lagoons.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are park entrance tickets included?

No. Park entrance tickets are not included in any Yala package, including transfers and the cruise-port tour. Allow about €35 (LKR 13,000) per person for park admission. Confirm the current fee with Janaka.

Which safari does Janaka recommend for leopard sightings?

Janaka suggests the Full Day or Special Leopard and Elephants safari, both of which give you an extended morning in the park.

Wildlife movements and conditions determine whether you see a leopard.

Are meals included in my safari?

The Full Day and Special Leopard and Elephants trips include breakfast. A Sri Lankan vegetarian lunch comes with the Full Day trip. Arrange meals separately on the Evening Safari.

How do I enquire about a safari?

Choose Enquire Now to open WhatsApp. Include your date, the safari you prefer, the number of adults, each child’s age and your hotel.
