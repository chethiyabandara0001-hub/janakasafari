# Sri Lanka Yala Safaris

This ZIP contains a static recreation of the 12 public pages in the Janaka Safari Jeep Tours sitemap, captured on 9 November 2026.

## Open the site

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Keep the HTML files and the `assets` folder together if you move or host the site.

## Source files

- `index.html`, `about.html`, `safari-parks.html`, `gallery.html`, `reviews.html` and `contact.html`
- Six park pages for Yala, Udawalawe, Bundala, Wilpattu, Minneriya and Kumana
- `assets/css` for shared styles and individual page styles
- `assets/js/site.js` for the navigation, photo viewer, review carousels and enquiry form
- `assets/media` and `assets/fonts` for local images and fonts

The site layout, park pages and photo viewer work offline. WhatsApp, email, hotel and review links open the relevant outside service. The enquiry form validates the fields and prepares a draft that the visitor can send by email or WhatsApp. Review cards use summaries from the captured reference and link to the source reviews. The Safari Videos section appears as a heading on the reference site.

The reference site provides the original visual design, brand and safari photographs. Some body copy is rephrased. Font notices are bundled in `assets/fonts`. [Janaka Safari Jeep Tours](https://www.srilankayalasafaris.com/)
