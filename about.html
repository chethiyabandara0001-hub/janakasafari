Meet Nisha & the Team

Safari Nisha and his team have guided trips in Yala since 2002. They founded Nisha Safari Jeep Tours in 2006.

Today, the team runs 4×4 trips in Yala, Udawalawe, Bundala, Wilpattu, Minneriya and Kumana. Yala is where their work began.

Ask the team to help choose a park and tour for your dates, whether you hope to see leopards, elephants or birds. Encounters depend on the wildlife and conditions on the day.
